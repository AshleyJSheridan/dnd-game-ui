Features

  • Character creation with name generator
  • Custom item creation
  • Random dice roller integrated into character sheet
  • Encounter creation by party or custom character levels
  • Campaign management
    • Upload as many maps as needed for a campaign
    • Upload docs, links, and add text snippets in Markdown to support a campaign
    • Manage players in a campaign, and send invites to prospective players
  • Multiplayer maps
    • Multiple maps per campaign
    • Interactive for all players in a campaign
    • Creature and character tokens
    • Drawings to illustrate area of effect spells or marked regions
    • Set the active map to prevent players accessing hidden areas
    • Hide creature tokens to create realistic scenarios for your players
